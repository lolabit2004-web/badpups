Brand Guidelines

  • You cannot use our logos or branding on projects, apparel, or merchandise without permission from our Community and Brand Manager: Contact us first.
  • Do not distort or alter BadPups logos – keep them as they are. If you need to resize them, make them smaller with the same original proportions (i.e. resize by dragging from a corner of the logo to keep the proportions the same).
  • Use empty space (whitespace) to keep our logo from touching other things, and also to give space between and around text.
  • Don’t confuse or mislead people – make sure information is clear and BadPups’ involvement is clear. Don’t use BadPups branding and designs to make it seem like your project is part of BadPups.
  • Don’t use the BadPups logos or banners as page/backgrounds for your design. Use plain colors and put text and logos on top.
  • If you need to have a background for your design (i.e. a poster) that has various colors, patterns, shapes, or an image, either have a section of solid color to put logos and text on top of, OR add an overlay over your background to darken/lighten it before adding text/images so the text and images are easier to read over the background.
  • If you are unsure if your use of our logos or brand resources meet our guidelines, contact us.

Style

  • BadPups branded materials should be white text and titles on a BadPups brand dark grey background.
  • Special information (use sparingly) and calls to actions should be in bold (still white text), OR BadPups brand red text.
  • In instances where white text on a dark background is not possible, reverse the order; Black text on a white or light color background. Where possible, do not use colored backgrounds – use white, black, or BadPups brand dark grey only.
