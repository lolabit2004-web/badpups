Beware of common scams and fakes

  • If you are being urged to take urgent action such as changing a password, paying, or providing verification documents, it is most likely a scam.
  • Fake copycat sites may imitate BadPups. They often have broken features, broken links, or details that look wrong.
  • If you are not expecting an email from BadPups, check the sender address against the official domains above and report suspicious messages.
  • BadPups Staff will not threaten to remove or ban you until you pay for gear, send documents through unofficial channels, or comply with an unusual demand.
